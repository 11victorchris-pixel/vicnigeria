Why digitise birth registration?

Five practical benefits this prototype demonstrates. Each one is about better record-keeping — not about replacing official government processes.

Easier access

Registration information is available through a clear portal instead of travelling to search a paper file.

Better organisation

Every record uses the same fields — name, date of birth, state, guardian — so files stay consistent.

Faster search & retrieval

Find a record instantly by child’s name or registration ID instead of paging through registers.

Less paper dependence

Digital copies reduce the risk of lost, damaged, or duplicated paper records.

Planning potential

Organised, countable records could one day help plan schools, clinics, and public services.

Honest scope

This demo stores fictional records in your browser only. It issues demo IDs — never official certificate numbers.
